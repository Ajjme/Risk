## Techniques and Methods Used

- **Gumbel Distribution**
  - Calculation and application of the Gumbel distribution for extreme value analysis
  - Example: Probability of rare flood events or maximum annual rainfall

- **Gamma Distribution**
  - Fitting and statistical inference using the Gamma distribution
  - Used for modeling rainfall amounts, insurance losses, or time-to-event data

- **Monte Carlo Simulation**
  - Generating random samples to model uncertainty and variability in systems
  - Example: Simulation of loss distributions and analysis of simulated outcomes

- **Trace Plots**
  - Visualization of parameter sampling in Monte Carlo and Bayesian methods
  - Checking convergence and diagnostics of simulation methods

- **Descriptive Statistics**
  - Calculation of mean, median, standard deviation, quantiles, and probability-weighted moments

- **Frequency and Severity Models**
  - Separating the modeling of how often events happen (frequency) versus how large they are when they occur (severity)

- **Parameter Estimation**
  - Maximum Likelihood Estimation (MLE) for distribution parameters
  - Moment estimation methods

- **Goodness-of-Fit Testing**
  - Assessing how well a particular distribution fits observed data (e.g., QQ plots, KS tests)

- **Visualization of Distributions**
  - Plotting PDFs, CDFs, and histograms for analytical and fitted distributions
